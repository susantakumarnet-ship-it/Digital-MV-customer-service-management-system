# Phase 3 Report: Public Complaint Registration and Complaint Management

**Status:** complete, accepted by the project owner on 13 November 2026
**Date:** 13 November 2026

## 1. What was delivered

| Area | Delivered |
|---|---|
| **Public complaint form** (`/report`, no login) | Five steps: your details, location and asset, request, attachments and consent, review. Dropdowns cascade Region › City › Site › Asset › Equipment type, showing active records only, and choosing an asset fills in its equipment type. Each step validates as you go, and server errors are shown on the right step. The received time is the server's time. A confirmation screen shows the reference (with a copy button), the received time with its timezone, safe contact guidance, and a notice if the same person recently reported the same site. |
| **Anti-spam and duplicate submissions** (PUB-05) | Per-IP hourly limit (configurable), hidden honeypot field, minimum fill time enforced with a signed form token, and optional Cloudflare Turnstile (D-32). An idempotency key means a retried or double-clicked submission returns the original reference instead of creating a second complaint. |
| **References** (BR-001) | `MV-YYYY-000001`, generated atomically on the server inside the same transaction as the complaint (D-13). |
| **Acknowledgement and alerts** | An email with the reference goes to the reporter and never includes internal notes. Supervisors whose data scope covers the site get an in-app alert. |
| **Attachments** (HO §7.1, SEC-05) | Allowed file types and size limits come from configuration (D-20: JPG, PNG, HEIC, PDF, DOCX, XLSX; 10 MB per file; 10 files and 50 MB per record). The real content type is checked from the file's bytes, so a renamed file is rejected. File names are sanitised and files are hashed with SHA-256. Storage is private: a local folder outside the web root in development, or any S3-compatible bucket. Downloads use signed links that expire after 5 minutes, issued only after checking the user may see the complaint; downloads are audited and forced to download rather than open in the browser. An optional ClamAV virus-scan hook is included (D-23). Staff can mark a file internal, and customers never see internal files. |
| **Internal and portal registration** | Staff record phone, email, walk-in and other complaints with the real received time (which cannot be in the future). Portal customers register for their own organisation only; the source is recorded as Customer Portal and the time as server time. Form dropdowns only offer sites inside the user's scope. |
| **Complaint queue** (CMP-01) | Quick tabs: Open, New, Under review, Awaiting customer, Overdue and All. Search, plus filters for priority, request type, region › city › site, received dates and unclassified. Sorting and paging. Filters are kept in the URL. CSV export is audited, masked for management viewers, and protected against spreadsheet formula injection. |
| **Complaint detail** (CMP-02) | Header with status, priority and overdue flags. Action buttons are generated by the workflow engine. Panels cover customer and contact, location and asset, the request, classification, editing intake details (with a reason, audited), attachments, the message thread with the customer, internal notes, linked duplicates, status history and the audit trail. |
| **Review and classification** (CMP-03) | Request type, priority, category, supervisor (defaults to the reviewer), asset or equipment correction, and review notes. Classifying a new complaint starts the review automatically and records the acknowledgement. **The SLA due date is the received time plus the priority's resolve-hours.** Overdue complaints are flagged in lists and on dashboards. |
| **Workflow actions** (WF-02) | Start review, request information (with a message to the customer), information received, mark as duplicate, reject and cancel. Mandatory reasons are enforced. Steps the system performs itself, and steps from later phases, cannot be triggered from outside. |
| **Duplicates** (BR-003) | Suggestions come from the same site (with same-asset and same-email flags), plus a search. The link must point to a valid original: not the complaint itself, not another duplicate, not a rejected or cancelled complaint. **Both records are kept**, and the original lists its duplicates. |
| **Request for information** (D-31) | The customer gets an email and, if they have a portal account, an in-app notice. Portal customers reply in the app with optional files, which returns the complaint to Under Review and alerts the supervisor. Public reporters reply to the support contact. |
| **Access and privacy** (RBAC-05, HO §11) | Every list, detail, export, download and dropdown endpoint is limited to the user's scope, and records outside it return *not found*. Contact details are masked for management viewers. Customers never see internal notes, internal files or staff actions. |
| **Dashboards** | Admin and management: complaint KPIs (total, received today, new, open, awaiting customer, overdue) and charts by month, status (with your configured labels), priority and region. Supervisor: KPIs, a review queue and an overdue list. Customer: complaints awaiting their information and their open complaints. |
| **API** | 69 documented paths ([openapi.json](../api/openapi.json)). New: `/public/*`, `/complaints/*` and `/files/*`. Migration `002` creates the complaints, attachments and idempotency collections with the indexes from HO §8.2. |

**New Phase 3 automated coverage**

- **Public form:**
  - bootstrap and cascading dropdowns (inactive records hidden; other master data not exposed)
  - a full submission: reference, file storage, organisation taken from the site, equipment type taken from the asset, acknowledgement email, supervisor alert, audit entry
  - validation messages; mandatory consent; an outdated privacy notice is refused
  - every link in the location chain is re-checked on the server
- **Anti-spam:** honeypot; too-fast and forged form tokens; a retried submission returns the same reference and creates nothing, and reusing its key with a different payload returns 422; the duplicate notice goes only to the same reporter; disabling the form returns 404.
- **Uploads:** spoofed content, blocked extensions, file-count and size limits, file-name sanitising.
- **Registration and scope:** staff and portal registration rules; scoped form dropdowns; engineer, customer and management scoping; out-of-scope access returns 404; contact masking; internal notes and actions hidden from customers.
- **Classification and workflow:** classification sets the SLA due date and is audited; invalid supervisor; overdue filter and summary; a mandatory reason plus a customer email on rejection, after which edits are blocked; the full request-for-information round trip; automatic and not-yet-built steps cannot be forced; duplicate rules, with both records kept.
- **Downloads and export:** signed downloads return the exact bytes; tampered, expired and swapped links are rejected; internal files are hidden; CSV export is protected against formula injection, masked and audited; intake edits are audited.
- **Browser journey:** a public submission through all five steps, with validation messages and the consent check, ending in a reference. A supervisor finds it, classifies it (setting the due date) and requests information. The portal customer sees the request and replies, and the complaint returns to review. An engineer outside the region gets *not found*. The public form passes the accessibility scan and fits a phone screen.

**Defects found and fixed during Phase 3 verification**

- **Responses missed just-uploaded files.** Several actions built their response inside the transaction, so new files weren't in the reply. Responses are now built after commit.
- **Management couldn't export complaints**, although the brief allows them to export reports. `complaint.export` was added to their role; exports stay masked and audited.
- **Customers could see every organisation's sites** when registering through the portal, and the page would fail if the public form was switched off. There is now a scoped options endpoint.
- **The status chart ignored configured labels.** It now uses them (D-01).
- **Upload security:** `file-type` was upgraded to v22 to fix an infinite-loop vulnerability (K-06).

## 4. Walkthrough (demo script)

Prerequisite: `npm run seed:demo` against your database. It adds 5 demo complaints and the demo users.

1. Open http://localhost:5610/login and click **Submit a complaint online**.
2. Fill in the form. Choose North Region › Northfield › Northfield Primary Substation, then an asset, and see the equipment type fill in. Try a 5-character description and see the validation message. Attach a photo. Try submitting without consent, then accept it and submit. Note the reference, and find the acknowledgement email in `apps/api/var/mail`.
3. Submit again with the same email and site. The confirmation now mentions your earlier reference.
4. Sign in as `supervisor@example.com`. The dashboard shows the new complaint in the review queue, and the bell shows an alert.
5. Open the complaint. Classify it (priority *Critical*) and see the due date appear. Add an internal note. Download the photo; the link stops working after 5 minutes.
6. Click **Request information** and type a message for the customer.
7. Sign in as `customer@example.com`. The dashboard shows the complaint as awaiting their information. Open it: there are no internal notes and no staff buttons. Reply, attaching a file. The status returns to *Under Review*.
8. As the supervisor, open another complaint for the same site and click **Mark as duplicate**. Pick the original from the suggestions and give a reason. Both complaints remain, and they are linked to each other.
9. **Complaints list:** try the Overdue tab, the filters and **Export CSV**. Sign in as `management@example.com` and export again; contact details show as *(masked)*.
10. Sign in as `engineer2@example.com` (South region) and open a North complaint's URL. You get *not found*.
11. **Configuration → Public form:** switch the form off. `/report` becomes unavailable, while portal registration still works.
